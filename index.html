What is solarpunk?

Solarpunk is a literary, art, and activist movement that envisions a sustainable, egalitarian future where humanity lives in harmony with nature, powered by renewable energy. Solarpunk is also a set of design principles based on eco activism and community resilience, emphasizing decentralized networks, mutual aid, and local self-sufficiency to withstand environmental and economic crises.
